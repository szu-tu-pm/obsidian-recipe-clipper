Orchard Breakfast Bake

  • 3 cups rolled oats
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 apples
  • 1 cup pears
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup walnuts
  • 3 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp flour
  1. Combine the oat mixture.
  2. Bake until the fruit is soft.
