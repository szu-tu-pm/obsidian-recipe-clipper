Root Vegetable Tray

  • 1 1/2 lb red potatoes
  • 2 1/4 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1¾ cups sliced carrots
  • 2-3 parsnips
  • 1 to 2 onions
  • 2 pounds (900 g) sweet potatoes
  • 1 cup (240ml) vegetable stock
  • 2 Tbsp. balsamic vinegar
  • 4 oz. mushrooms
  • 5 fl oz apple cider
  • 1 fl. oz. lemon juice
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  • a pinch chili flakes
  • salt to taste
  • olive oil as needed
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 1 onion, cut into wedges
  • 250 g beets
  • 2 cups spinach
  1. Cut the vegetables evenly.
  2. Roast until browned.
