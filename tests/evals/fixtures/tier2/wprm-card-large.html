Root Vegetable Tray

  • 2 lb potatoes
  • 1 lb carrots
  • 8 oz parsnips
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 onion
  • 1 cup mushrooms
  • 250 g beets
  • 1 bunch rosemary
  • 2 tbsp vinegar
  • 1/2 cup stock
  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 4 cups spinach
  1. Cut the vegetables evenly.
  2. Roast until browned.
