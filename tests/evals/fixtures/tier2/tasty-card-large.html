Skillet Bean Supper

  • 2 cans white beans
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 250 ml broth
  • 100 g kale
  • 1 cup corn
  • 2 tbsp parsley
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 4 oz noodles
  1. Soften the onion and garlic.
  2. Simmer the beans and noodles.
