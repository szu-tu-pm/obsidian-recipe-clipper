RDFa Pantry Salad

  • 2 cups chickpeas
  • 1 cup cucumber
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 100 g radishes
  • 250 g tomatoes
  • 1 bunch mint
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 can corn
  • 4 oz feta
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cups lettuce
  • 30 ml vinegar
  1. Whisk the dressing.
  2. Toss the vegetables and serve.
