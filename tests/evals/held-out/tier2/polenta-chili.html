Polenta with Chili Crisp

  • 2 1/2 cups polenta
  • 3 1/4 cups buckwheat
  • ½ cup miso
  • ⅓ cup maple syrup
  • 6 to 7 cans artichokes
  • 7-8 thyme leaves
  • 6 ounces (170g) soba
  • 4 cups (960 ml) dashi
  • 2 Tbsp. black vinegar
  • ½ c. teff
  • 3 fl oz yuzu juice
  • 10 fl oz barley tea
  • 5 sprigs dill
  • 6 sprigs tarragon
  • fish sauce as needed
  • garnish as desired
  • 1 fennel bulb, coarsely chopped
  • 2 plums, thinly sliced
  • 320 g black beans
  • 400 g potatoes
  1. Stir the polenta until thick.
  2. Spoon the chili crisp over the top.
